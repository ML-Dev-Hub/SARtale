Questions and limits

The questions people ask most, with straight answers.

Is SARtale a flood warning system?

No. It is indicative screening on provisional data. It shows whether a lake's water area has changed against its own noise. Official warnings come from PMD, NDMA, GBDMA and PDMA.

Why 11 lakes and not all 131 that SUPARCO tracks?

Each lake needs a coordinate with a published source and enough NISAR passes on one track. The 11 were chosen for their sources and their history, with Attabad as a check on the method. The method is the same for any lake, and anyone can put one forward with "Add a lake". The team adds it once its source checks out.

Can radar really see through cloud?

Yes. NISAR's L-band microwaves pass through cloud and need no sunlight, so it sees the lakes day or night. From 17 June to 4 August 2026, while cloud limited optical images, NISAR made 60 valid looks across the 11 lakes.

Do mountain shadows hide lakes?

They can, for both kinds of satellite. Radar looks sideways, so a slope facing away from it falls in radar shadow and looks as dark as water. SARtale compares the views from the two directions and sets aside anything dark from only one. If less than half of a lake's zone can be seen, it reads Not observable instead of a guess.

Why do radar and Sentinel-2 disagree so much?

At the four lakes checked, radar water and optical water overlapped poorly (IoU 0.013 to 0.09). Optical water indices also flag shadow and wet ground, radar misses wind-roughened water and shadowed shores, and even on the same day the two satellites pass at different hours. Neither is a clean reference, which is why the app calls its status indicative.

How current is the data?

As current as the last pipeline run. The latest pass in this build is 23 October 2026. The story page and every lake show the date of their latest pass.

What does "provisional" mean?

NASA released these NISAR files (CRID P05023) before validation. NASA will reprocess them, and numbers may change when it does.

Known limits

  • A status is about water area only, not volume, depth or hazard.
  • Zones are approximate: an outline for Attabad, a 1 km circle elsewhere.
  • Frozen lakes, lakes under 0.05 km² and wind-roughened water are not validated.
  • The lake request server is built for a demo or a small pilot (see Deploying).
