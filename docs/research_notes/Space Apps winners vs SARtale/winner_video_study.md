# How winning Space Apps demos tell their story (studied 3 Nov 2026)

Videos and captions were downloaded with yt-dlp (default settings) into `data/winner_videos/` (git-ignored). Frames were sampled every 2.5 s. Two Drive-hosted 2025 demos (Astro Sweepers, Zumorroda-X) were studied from frames only. The ElevenLabs key has no speech-to-text permission.

Not studied:
- PureFlow, Greetings from Earth!! and Landsat Connect: YouTube reports them unavailable.
- A Solar Tale: needs a signed-in account.

## Per video

| Video (award) | Structure | Notable device |
|---|---|---|
| QUEÑARIS (Local Impact 2025), 30 s | name + one-line purpose; problem; solution in 3 verbs; traction; slogan | real local photos; "springs drop 14 L/s to zero in days"; "5,000 seedlings ready"; laptop mock-ups of each screen; close "Bringing back the tree of the Andes" |
| WaterWise (Data 2024), 31 s | product walkthrough, one caption per action | burned-in captions tell the story; ends on slogan "get your farm's water usage right" |

| Connected Earth Museum (Art & Tech 2024) | "Welcome to…", what you can explore, host character "Billy" | guide character |
| Aqua Explorer (Storytelling 2023), ~4 min | rhyme opener, team, character "Maya", stakes number, game walkthrough, call to action | character, a rhyme, "why does this matter?" |

| Zumorroda-X (Art & Tech 2025), 29 s, frames only | character Henry's "Farming Crisis", crisis scenarios, game screens with NASA maps | a character in crisis, then the tool |

## Common pattern of the 30-second winners

1. **Second 0–3:** the product name plus one plain line saying what it is for.
2. **Problem:** one local, physical, numeric fact with real imagery.
3. **Solution:** three verbs, each shown on a real screen, often in a laptop mock-up.
4. **Proof:** something already real (traction, partners, a number).
5. **Slogan close.**

Captions are large and carry the message even without sound. There is little or no method detail; that lives on the project page.
